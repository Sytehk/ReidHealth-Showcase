# Reid Health

**Healthcare operations and care coordination**

Reid Health is a healthcare services platform supporting home and community care, clinical support, staffing, and care-team operations.

## Project scope

- Care and administrative workflows
- Staff scheduling and time-off management
- Patient, provider, and office coordination

**Technology:** Next.js and TypeScript, Supabase and PostgreSQL.
